# DARKWIZ Construction Toolkit — changelog

## v1.2.0 — Versioning, new logo and reference notes (2026-09-30)
- New: version number under the Login button and next to your account in the top bar, with a link to this changelog.
- New: DARKWIZ CT logo (roof truss with a spark) on the login page, top bar and browser tab.
- New: footer link to darkwizlab.com.
- New: Reference notes library on the References page — search provisions, filter by code and topic, and keep your own notes.
- Changed: clause bookmarks are now part of Reference notes.
- Changed: time zones show their GMT offset, for example (GMT+8) Asia Manila, in Settings and on the clock.

## v1.1.1 — Renamed to DARKWIZ CT (2026-09-30)
- Changed: app renamed from DARKWIZ QS to DARKWIZ CT (Construction Toolkit). Backups made under the old name still import.

## v1.1.0 — Own website, mobile layout and quick quotations (2026-09-30)
- New: can be hosted on your own website with email and password login, password reset by email and an online database.
- New: phone layout with a bottom tab bar; installable on the home screen.
- New: Quick quotation in Quick tools with Excel (live formulas and cost breakdown) and PDF export.
- New: contract documents can store a link to the file.

## v1.0.0 — First release (2026-09-30)
- Dashboard with project-filtered reports, calendar, to-do list and personal notes.
- Create and open projects: PERT/CPM scheduling, Gantt chart, bill of quantities and materials, billing management, plan vs actual.
- Material, labour and equipment database with price history; procurement requests, suppliers and budgets.
- Quick tools: quick estimate and unit, strength, size, code and other conversions.
- References, and settings for units, display and project defaults.
